Overall Accuracy vs SNR

Overall classification accuracy across all 47 signal classes at each SNR level. Accuracy rises sharply from -5 dB (13%) to a peak at +5 dB (78%), then declines to a 58-61% plateau from +15 dB up — a non-monotonic curve, not yet the smooth monotonic rise we're aiming for. The high-SNR dip is concentrated in a handful of classes (see Hard Classes below) whose accuracy collapses above +10 dB; most classes remain near-100% at high SNR. Below -5 dB the problem becomes very hard — constellation structure collapses into noise.

Per-Class Accuracy (all SNRs mixed)

Accuracy for each of the 47 signal classes, averaged over all SNR levels from −10 to +30 dB. AM_SSB_LSB, LFM, POCSAG and ADS-B score highest due to their distinctive spectral signatures (single-sideband, chirp sweep, FSK paging tones, packet preamble). QAM64, QAM32, 16PSK, FM_WB and RTTY are the hardest — mostly constellation-order confusion within the PSK/QAM family, plus a high-SNR collapse for FM_WB and RTTY.

Values measured at mixed SNR (−10 to +30 dB). High-SNR-only would be ~15pp higher for most classes.

Hard Classes Analysis

Classes with accuracy below 50% are next in line for targeted improvement — 21 of 47 classes fall in this band. The classic PSK/QAM-order-confusion group (QAM64, QAM32, 16PSK, 32PSK, QAM256, QAM16) persists from earlier versions. A second group — several of the generator-fixed classes (ACARS, DTMF, AM_DSB, AM_DSB_SC, EAS_SAME, P25_C4FM, FLEX) plus FM_WB and RTTY — now produce structurally correct signals but show a sharp "high-SNR collapse": strong accuracy in a narrow SNR band, falling to near-zero above +10..+15 dB.

v9 (this page) fixed 8 generator bugs from v7 — Hermitian-symmetric-spectrum and unfiltered-audio issues in ACARS, DTMF, AM_DSB, AM_DSB_SC, AM_SSB_LSB/USB, EAS_SAME, MDC_1200 and P25_C4FM. Overall accuracy rose from 50.2% (v7) to 52.7% (v9), and 5 classes that were stuck at 0% under v7 (ACARS, DTMF, AM_DSB_SC, AM_SSB_LSB, AM_SSB_USB) now score 21-84%. The dominant remaining issue is the high-SNR collapse seen in several of these newly-fixed classes plus FM_WB/RTTY/AM_DSB — each performs well in a narrow SNR band (often 0..+10 dB) but falls toward 0% at +15 dB and above, which is what produces the non-monotonic overall SNR curve above. Next step: investigate whether these classes are being confused with a specific neighbour at high SNR, and consider extending HARD_CLASSES oversampling and the SNR augmentation range to cover this band.
