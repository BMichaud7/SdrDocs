Overall Accuracy vs SNR

Overall classification accuracy across all 47 signal classes at each SNR level. The model rises sharply from 0 dB (75%) to peak at +5 dB (83%), then settles to a 70-72% plateau from +10 dB up. Below 0 dB the problem becomes very hard — constellation structure collapses into noise.

Per-Class Accuracy (all SNRs mixed)

Accuracy for each of the 47 signal classes, averaged over all SNR levels from −10 to +30 dB. LFM, ADS-B, AM_DSB and POCSAG score highest due to their distinctive spectral signatures (chirp sweep, packet preamble, strong carrier, FSK paging tones). QAM64, 16PSK, QAM32, NAVTEX and 32PSK are the hardest — primarily due to constellation-order confusion within the PSK/QAM family.

Values measured at mixed SNR (−10 to +30 dB). High-SNR-only would be ~15pp higher for most classes.

Hard Classes Analysis

Classes with accuracy below 50% are next in line for targeted improvement. The common root causes are constellation-order overlap within the PSK/QAM family, structural confusion with neighbouring protocol classes, and a training-noise floor that under-represents genuinely clean (+20..+30 dB) signals.

Classes with accuracy < 50%

Sorted by accuracy ascending

QAM64 — 12.5%Worst class. Confused mostly with QAM256 — the model frequently mistakes the denser constellation for a higher-order one, especially at high SNR.
16PSK — 13.4%Confused with 8PSK and QPSK — classic PSK-order ambiguity, persists across all SNRs.
QAM32 — 21.2%Confused with QAM256 and QAM16 — same constellation-order overlap as QAM64.
NAVTEX — 31.6%FSK-based maritime telex — confused with RTTY and P25_PHASE2 at high SNR (similar tone-switching structure).
32PSK — 33.0%Confused with QPSK and 8PSK — same PSK-order ambiguity as 16PSK.
AM_DSB_SC — 37.6%Suppressed-carrier AM — overlaps with QAM256 and other amplitude-modulated digital signals at low SNR.
QAM256 — 43.7%High-order QAM — degrades at very high SNR, likely a training-noise-floor effect (the model rarely saw genuinely clean +30 dB QAM256 examples).
QPSK — 46.5%Confused with neighbouring PSK orders, though less severely than 16PSK/32PSK.
4FSK — 49.8%Accuracy collapses from 76.5% at 0 dB to ~3% at +15..+30 dB — a strong example of the high-SNR training-noise-floor issue.
TONE — 49.5%Single-tone carrier — confused with AM_DSB and OOK, which can look similar without modulation present.

v7 training (in progress) targets these specific failure modes: IqAugment's noise ceiling was raised from 30 dB to 40 dB SNR so the model sees more near-clean high-SNR examples (addressing 4FSK/QAM256/P25_C4FM-style high-SNR collapse), and HARD_CLASSES oversampling now also covers P25_C4FM, NXDN, DTMF, GFSK, FSK, TONE, 4FSK and 8FSK.
