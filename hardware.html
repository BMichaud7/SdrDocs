Selection Guide

Just getting started / ADS-B, FM, AIS, NOAA
RTL-SDR Blog v4 ($40). Limited to 1.77 GHz, 8-bit ADC, but covers 90% of common monitoring targets cheaply. Stack multiple dongles for parallel channels.
Multi-protocol monitoring, P25, GPS, cellular
PlutoSDR ($250) or LimeSDR Mini 2 ($300). 12-bit ADC, full duplex, coverage up to 3.5–3.8 GHz. PlutoSDR has a larger SoapySDR community; LimeSDR covers down to 10 MHz for HF protocols.
Direction finding (MUSIC algorithm)
USRP B210 ($2,100). Only device in this list with two coherent Rx channels required for phase-based DF. Alternatively, use two synchronized PlutoSDRs with external 10 MHz reference — contact the OpenRFStack team for the dual-device DF configuration.
Full 6 GHz coverage, protocol research
HackRF One ($340). Widest frequency range, but 8-bit and half-duplex. Not suitable for simultaneous Tx/Rx or weak-signal classification — use for survey sweeps and initial protocol identification.
